The strongest objection, as a theorem

This section's ledger has treated the human-essential reservoir as a judgment; this subsection prices it. Let K ⊂ [0,1], of measure k > 0, be the tasks machines cannot hold — for any reason: capability residue, enforced preference, law — earning wage wK, and let per-task machine costs m collapse along Section 5's margins.

Proposition 9 (The Baumol fork). (i) Every good whose checklist meets K has unit cost C = k·wK/γL + (1−k)·m → k·wK/γL as m → 0, and labor’s share of that good’s cost → 1: Baumol’s disease, derived rather than assumed. (ii) Against K-light substitutes with elasticity η, the expenditure share of K-content tends to 1 for η < 1, to the taste weight θ at η = 1, and to 0 for η > 1: the rescue forks on substitutability, not on sentiment. (iii) Terminal expenditure concentrates on the non-collapsing categories — direct land services and K-labor — with the machine-made share at zero; labor’s aggregate share is bounded below by the K-share on the η < 1 branch, and the corner regime of Propositions 3–8 is exactly the K = ∅ boundary of this theorem.

Proof. (i) Read the share off C(m) and let m → 0. (ii)–(iii) CES shares with one collapsing price: the collapsing term vanishes from the denominator for η < 1 and dominates it for η > 1; with three categories the machine term vanishes and land and K split the limit by weights and prices, θT·r1−η against θK·wK1−η; setting the K-weight to zero returns the corner. ∎

Remark (what K is made of, and what it shrinks to). Membership has three sources: capability residue (branch one of this section’s first entry — thinning, by that fork’s own argument), preference, and law. A preference-based membership must be verifiable to bind: at the android limit human provenance is a credence good, and the sustainable premium obeys πH ≤ min(φH, v·f/(1−v)) — latent taste φH, verification power v, legal penalty f for provenance fraud. Enforced as price alone (f → 0), the premium collapses for every v < 1: certified-human unravels exactly as Proposition 2’s self-liquidation predicts, through fakes rather than robots. The two survivable anchors are f > 0 — provenance law, which is quantity exclusion in Section 4’s sense, the fortified set again — and v → 1: co-presence, humanity verified by being in the room. So K’s terminal core is what law reserves plus what bodies witness; the floor under human premia is a property of the proof apparatus over tasks, not of the tasks.

Remark (superstars: the rescue is distributionally hollow). Within K, reach is machine-side: recording, broadcast, replication serve the β-fraction of K-demand that does not require the room, and concentrate it on the best few, while the co-present remainder is rationed by human capacity. With n K-workers, the top performer’s share of K-income is β + (1−β)/n — rising one-for-one with reach — and the median K-income falls to (1−β) of the mean. Combine with the fork: on the η < 1 branch the aggregate labor share can persist, even approach one, while the median wage still collapses to the floor — the persisting share belongs to K, and within K to its stars. Aggregate rescue, median demolition: the objection, even granted its premise, does not save the paycheck it was raised to defend.

One consequence travels back to Section 6: on the K branch the subsistence bundle itself carries a K-service component — care, teaching, the human hour the floor must buy — so Ps gains a non-collapsing term and the coverage ratio κ climbs more slowly at every q. The reservation economy taxes its own remedy.
