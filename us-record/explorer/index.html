How much each number matters, here

Each number is nudged down and up from where it is now, with everything else held, and the five misfits are compared. Numbers searched in logs move 5%, rates half a point, a point's year one year. The squares are the five series (wage in durables, wage in shelter, labour's share, prime-age employment, labour's share of a machine's cost): red worse, green better, blank under 5%. At a best fit the total barely moves either way, so the squares tell more: they show what each number trades against what.
