# Published numbers of the 2026-06-14 post

`post_published_numbers.csv` lists every number printed in Stella's post of 2026-06-14, "Labor pressures causing market distortion and a minimally invasive solution": the body text, figure captions and footnotes (`../raw/post/post.txt`, extracted from `post.html`) and the eleven figures at full resolution (`../raw/post/figures/`). It also holds values read off unlabelled figure elements: ghost outlines, hollow diamonds, stacked bar segments, path lines, band edges and point positions. The rebuild of the post's model (15-point VAT surcharge funding a flat per-adult dividend; Finland HBS 2022 by quintile, US CE 2024 by decile) uses it as the list of numbers it has to reproduce.

One row per printed number; a printed range such as "7–9%" gives two rows. 1168 rows.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `id` | P001, P002, ... P001–P932 are in order of appearance in the post, with text and figures interleaved as published. P933–P1168 were added by the check below; they are in order of appearance among themselves. |
| `source` | `text`, or the figure's file name |
| `locator` | Text: the paragraph's first six words (footnotes start with their marker, e.g. `[^20]:`). Figure: the element (`panel title`, `bar label`, `point label`, `axis tick`, `legend`, `annotation`, `footnote`, `box text`; `... (read off axis)` for unlabelled elements). |
| `item` | category or series name exactly as printed |
| `group` | country prefix (`FI`, `US`) with quintile or decile (`Q1`, `D10`), `all`, scenario (`lambda=0.35`) and year where they apply |
| `quantity` | what the number measures |
| `value` | a plain number; negative for falls, net payments and withdrawals |
| `unit` | `EUR`, `USD`, `%`, `pp`, `ratio`, `year` (calendar year or ramp-year index), `years` (duration), `count`, ... |
| `printed` | the exact printed string; empty for values read off the axis |
| `confidence` | `high` = printed as text; `medium` = read off a bar, line or point position; `low` = uncertain reading |
| `note` | how a read-off was made, links between text and figures, discrepancies |

**How it was made.** One transcription pass: an agent read `post.txt` and each figure, and measured unlabelled elements in pixels against the tick marks. Then the independent check of 2026-11-02 below: the whole text and every figure were read again, every read-off value was measured again, missing numbers were added and the descriptions were made consistent.

**Conventions.**

- The axes of Figures 7, 8 and 10 say "% of total", meaning total consumption; those rows say "% of consumption", as the text does.
- VAT surcharge rates are in `pp`, also where a figure prints "15%" or "τ(yr 2)=10%".
- Fraction words in the text ("two-thirds", "a quarter", "roughly a tenth") are recorded in `%`.
- The same quantity has the same `quantity` text in the text rows and the figure rows (for example `MPC=1 benchmark (ghost) value` for the Fig 2/5 outlines and the Fig 3/6 hollow diamonds). Text rows keep the item names the text prints (e.g. "new cars"); their notes give the figure category ("Cars and trucks, new").
- Read-off rows give their pixel scale and precision in the note. Bars and whisker caps snap to whole pixels, so their precision is about half a pixel at each end.

**Left out on purpose.** Identifiers and labels (figure, table, footnote, section and equation numbers, "Item 1" in the Fig 8/10 titles, COICOP codes inside category names, StatFin and BLS table codes, "Fable 5"); citation years and editions (except "2005 ICP Data", the only statement of the β parameters' data vintage); counts that only describe the essay's own structure or concepts ("four functions", "three mechanisms", "two channels", "three readings", "(i)–(iii)", "pick two", "taxed twice", "one-time levy", "works once"); idioms and figures of speech ("from the first day", "from the first month", "LVT-from-day-one", "one explicit fight ... ten thousand recurring ones", "a krona of earnings", "a euro of evasion"); and the site's "More posts" list.

## Transcription check (2026-11-02)

**Method.** Every paragraph of `post.txt` was read again, and every text row was checked automatically: its locator is the first six words of a paragraph, and its printed string occurs verbatim in that paragraph. Every figure was read again at full resolution, with zoomed crops of the label areas. Every read-off value (Fig 1/4 point positions and break-even lines, Fig 2/5 ghost outlines, Fig 3/6 diamonds, Fig 7 plateaus and bands, Fig 8/10 paths, band edges, bars and net markers, Fig 9 whisker caps) was measured again from the PNG pixels against a fit through the tick marks.

**Findings.**

- Printed numbers: no misreadings. Every printed value, sign and digit in the 932 rows matches the text or the figure label it cites.
- Read-off values: three were off and are corrected: P518 (Fig 7 80%-pass-through edge, 7.41 → 7.39; 7.41 would lie inside the solid fill), P598 (Fig 8 full A-K path at year 1, −0.96 → −0.95) and P516 (Fig 7 central plateau, 8.33 → 8.32, a borderline case). All other read-offs reproduce within their stated precision.
- Representation: P004 and P012 recorded "mid-1950s" and "mid-2000s" as 1950 and 2000 (now 1955, 2005); P569 had the opposite sign convention to P042 for the same wealth revaluation (now −8); P023 and P025 used unit `share` where all other fraction words use `%`; P077, P288 and P792 gave the surcharge in `%` instead of `pp`; the c_w rows P618, P791 and P840 had unit "per dollar per year", which is wrong for Finland (now `per year`).
- Consistency: "% of total" vs "% of consumption" for the same demand paths, bands and bars; different `quantity` texts for the same value in Fig 2/5 and Fig 3/6 and in text vs figure rows; lambda rows with the scenario in the quantity or in free-text groups; empty groups on 80 axis-tick rows; and smaller naming differences (pass-through, price level, labour-market effects, cited statistics). All harmonised; see the log.
- Missing numbers, 236 rows added: 27 numbers in the text (time spans and dates such as "half a century", "within seven years", "a decade-plus", "within the first year", the footnote years in notes 15, 22, 28, 31 and 33, "2005 ICP Data", "zero share", "supply elasticity zero", "the automatable half", "these five points"); 59 axis ticks of Figures 1–6 and 9 (the first pass recorded ticks for Figures 7, 8 and 10 only); and 150 unlabelled stacked segments of Figures 3 (quintiles, P975–P1024) and 6 (deciles, P1050–P1149). The first pass had read the stacked bars of Figures 8 and 10 but not these. Each set of segments sums to its net within one pixel.

**Counts.** Rows checked: 932. Rows changed: 310 (369 field changes). Rows added: 236. Rows now: 1168.

**Discrepancies inside the post** (kept as printed; the notes cross-reference them):

**Still uncertain.**

- P887: the unit of observation of '$6.6M' (top-decile net worth) is not stated (household, presumably).

- Values close to zero that are read off the axis (Fig 2/5 ghosts within ±0.02 of zero, Fig 9 whisker caps next to the zero line) carry ±0.01; Fig 3/6 segments under 5 px wide are marked low.
- Fig 8 right panel, years 3-5: the levy and smoothing bars run past the axis minimum, so only the top of the grey segment can be read (P628-P630); the sign of the smoothing component (P626-P627, P851-P855) is inferred from net = flow + levy + smoothing.
- P306: the D2 point in Fig 4 is hidden behind D4; its x-position is implied by the line (low).
- Scope: the numbers excluded by the rules above (essay-structure counts, figures of speech, identifiers, citation years) are left out on purpose; say if the rebuild needs any of them.
