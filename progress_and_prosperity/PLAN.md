## The papers

Sketches thin from P1 to P5 on purpose, same fade as the phases.

P1 — Pinning the Wage to Scarcity and Technology. Exists (SSRN working draft, Aug 2026); the anchor everything else cites. Contains: the task margin w = c·ρ(x*); the replacement closure through the machine recipe; priced exit s(q); the interval closed from both ends; the flat-capability limit and the real-wage fork; the rent-tax + uniform-dividend welfare pair; history as three configurations; the measurements (fork 4.8×, κ 0.33 [0.18–0.59], fiscal exposure) and the thirteen-entry register; appendices A–I. Queued revisions: a human-capital subsection in the standing-accounts survey; a provisioning-identity section (the fiscal-exposure result promoted from appendix to spine — see P2); Lean coverage extended to λ > 0; whatever referees and readers force.

P2 — The transition. Question: which bases bridge the crossing, at what deadweight, on what timetable. Theory core (seeded in a ChatGPT-side thread — credit it in the AI-use note): the provisioning identity — the consolidated base Y_S = wL + τR + B whose composition the transition migrates (B, the borrowed channel, is 19% of transfers and rising, so the two-term version is the limit case only) — and the endogenous dependency floor: private support as a claim on the same wage the model determines. Done carefully: endogenize only the private channel so Prop 5's Δ = 0 cancellation survives; the resulting backward-bending participation gives a twin-equilibrium result (comfortable vs collapse) that formalizes F.4's race. Couples to P4 through the support ratio. Skeleton otherwise exists as the 06/14 analysis. Contains: the funding taxonomy on the way down (rent base first, VAT bridge, the λC(1−κ)² deadweight index); the Auerbach–Kotlikoff one-time-levy reading of the VAT step, with the λ-perception parameter and the US/Nordic calibrations; buy-side sovereign fund vs the Meidner share levy; pensions in full — the notional-vs-funded fork, keep-promises-migrate-base, the double-payment problem, κ-keyed mandate decay, Sweden NDC and Norway as the two precedents (the ownership-diffusion reading of the funded pillar hands off to P5); conditionality extended (F.1's three failure modes); the enclosure race and the fundable-floor timing (F.4); trigger design — phases keyed to measured series (pass-through, κ, λ), not dates. Data machinery largely built (Finnish HBS, US CE, Z.1/κ); pension-system accounts to add.

P3 — Capability, education, experience. Question: how human capability investment — schooling before the market, experience inside it — races the flattening front. Contains: Blocks A–D grown up (talent/practice split; machine mirror with flattening as a named assumption; the education race and its hump-shaped premium path; μ anatomy, five species, displacement-loss decomposition); the junior extension — entry-rung tasks flip first, experience-production automated at the input stage, seniors as a quasi-terminal input on depletion-scheduled rents, the experience premium running the education premium's hump one phase later, Becker's tuition-by-busywork channel collapsing at a zero busywork price, junior hiring as a market-implied capability forecast; the credence application — grades as human-capability certificates under the fraud bound, co-presence as the anchor. Data: the MORG premium-race series (pulled; pass two is the Goldin–Katz adjustment), early-career employment in exposed occupations, the task-birth series. Split decision (C+experience vs μ anatomy) deferred until the weight is known.

P4 — The demographic closure. Question: close the loop from rents to population and back to the floor's feasibility. Household formation is already the model's exit margin (prediction 13); fertility sits downstream of it; N sits in κ's denominator and its physical ceiling. The inversion of Malthus: classical regime, population adjusts to the wage floor; terminal regime, population adjusts through priced household formation — and falling N relaxes the land constraint. Aging feeds the fiscal scissors. The support ratio in P2's endogenous floor is where this paper plugs in. Data direction: formation/coresidence panels against local rent-to-wage; fertility against rent exposure. Thin sketch; firms up in Phase 2.

P5 — The feasibility frontier. Same identity as P2, distributional view: P2 asks what each instrument does to Y_S; P5 asks who sits on which side of it — who votes for τ > 0, and when. Consumes P2's incidence engine as input, so it ships after P2, and merges into P2 or into the book's chapter 10 if it turns out thin — decide at delivery, not now. Contains: ownership dynamics through the transition (Prop 5's path starts from the inherited distribution); the net-beneficiary share as a function of τ computed from wealth-survey microdata; offsets-not-exemptions worked as design (dividend covers the median parcel; deferral-to-estate); the funded-pension diffusion result; the loop-raises-rents increment as the dynamic on-ramp; new-rents-first sequencing (AI-buildout claims have no incumbent voter majority).

λ series (standalone short empirical paper — the gate). Question: is the labor content of machine production falling? Define the empirical counterpart of the matrix recursion (labor share of machine-producing and -operating sectors; resolution of machine-intensive output into wages vs terminal rents after recursively removing intermediates); build the time series from BEA IO accounts and OECD ICIO; all defensible classification variants with bands; state the falsification reading both ways. Built once; where it's reported is decided at delivery — default is both: a results subsection in P1's Section 10 revision (your fold-in instinct) plus a short companion note, so the series is citable by people who reject the theory and the gate never waits on P1's revision cycle.

Data notes (stay notes unless they grow): the incidence-slope assembly (payroll pass-through estimates on one common definition, read as the schedule's slope over time); the long record (welfare ratios against land shares across the two regime changes); international κ (EU replication, China scoping).
