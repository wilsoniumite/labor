# Highlights

- Wages are pinned to the cost of labor's machine substitute and to exit options
- Automation works through better machines and through less labor in making them
- Both channels can lower wages relative to rents on land and other scarce inputs
- Embodied rents cap wage purchasing power; feasible human production sets a floor
- U.S. wages in durables and in shelter diverged by a factor of 4.8 in 1964–2024
