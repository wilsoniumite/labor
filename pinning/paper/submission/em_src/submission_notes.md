# Economic Modelling: submission checklist

Special issue "Artificial Intelligence, Data Investment and the Digital Economy". Deadline 28 February 2027.
Submit at https://www.editorialmanager.com/ECMODE/default.aspx and choose the article type
**VSI: AI, Data Investment and the Digital Economy**. Review is double-anonymized.

**Fee:** USD 125 at submission, non-refundable (the guide: a paper can still be desk-rejected).

## Files (build: `python3 code/build_submission_em.py`, output in `paper/submission/em/`)

| Upload as | File | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Manuscript (LaTeX source) | `manuscript.tex` | anonymized; compiles as is with the figures beside it |
| Manuscript (Word), only if the system asks for an editable file instead | `manuscript.docx` | the same anonymized text; Word equations, numbered as in LaTeX |
| Figures | `Figure_1.pdf` … `Figure_7.pdf`, `Figure_D1.pdf` | vector, fonts embedded |
| Title page | `title_page.docx` | author details, competing interests (the SEB employment), funding; no acknowledgments |
| Highlights | `highlights.docx` | 5 bullets, 78–80 characters each (limit 85) |
| Cover letter | `cover_letter.docx` | to the guest editors |
| Declaration of interest | from Elsevier's declarations tool | declare Stella Wilson's employment at SEB, as on the title page |

`manuscript_preview.pdf` is for checking only: the journal does not take a PDF as the source file.

## Form fields

- Title: Pinning the Wage to Scarcity and Technology: Replacement, exit, and the rents of non-produced inputs in a task economy
- Authors, in this order: Johan Båge; Stella Wilson (corresponding, thmwi@kth.se)
- Abstract: as in the manuscript (140 words; limit 250)
- Keywords (1–7): automation; artificial intelligence; wages; land rent; production networks; labor force participation; rent taxation
- JEL codes, if asked: J31, O33, E25, J22, R31, H21
- Funding: This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors.
- Data statement (draft): The data are public U.S. statistical series (BEA national accounts and fixed-asset accounts, FRED, Federal Reserve Z.1 Financial Accounts, and the BLS CPI); the article lists the series. The code and processed data that reproduce every figure and reported number are in a public repository; its link is left out of the manuscript during review to keep the review anonymous, is available to the editor on request, and will be cited in the article, with a DOI, on acceptance.
- Preprint posting on SSRN offered during submission: decline (already posted as SSRN 7226858).
- Generative AI: declared in the manuscript, in its own section before the references.
