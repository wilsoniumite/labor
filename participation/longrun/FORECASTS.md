# Long-run forecasts (filled before `participation-L-freeze`)

Each participant fills their own table before the tag, after reading `longrun/PROTOCOL.md` and the European
benchmark runs that are committed with the freeze. The sheets are committed with the tag and never edited after
it. They are reported beside the results. A verdict to which every forecaster gave at least 0.5 is marked "as
forecast" (`PROTOCOL.md`, section 5).

How to fill a row:
- **Expected sign:** the sign you expect the estimate to have (+ or −).
- **P(model's sign):** your probability that the point estimate has the model's sign.
- **P(supported), P(contradicted):** your probabilities of each verdict after Holm, the size correction and the
  randomisation co-primary. P(inconclusive) is 1 minus their sum.
- **Note:** one line, optional.

Leave the LR-C1 row empty and mark it "dropped" if she decides against Extract B before the freeze.
